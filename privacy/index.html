Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Your routines, habits and progress stay on your phone. Enact only receives what you share for reading, and keeps the resulting steps, not the original.

Who we are

Enact is operated by Sune Marcussen, Denmark, and is available worldwide. For anything about your data, email hello@enact.life.

What stays on your phone

Your routines, steps, settings, who you're becoming, run history, streaks and saved sources are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app, or Settings > Start over, deletes them.

Where data leaves the European Union, the transfer is covered by the provider's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis

Reading what you share is needed to provide the service you asked for. The usage log, anonymous statistics and crash reports rely on our legitimate interest in keeping Enact working and improving it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to how we use it. The next test version adds Settings > Delete my data, which removes everything linked to your account ID from our server. Until then, email hello@enact.life and we will help within 30 days. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Denmark that is Datatilsynet.

Age

Enact is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. Significant changes are shown in the app.
