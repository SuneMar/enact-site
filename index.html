Don't just watch it. Enact it.

Share a reel, a video or a podcast to Enact. It turns what you watched into small daily habits you can actually keep.

  1. Share it

    From Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or any app, tap Share and choose Enact it.

  2. Get the steps

    Enact reads what's said and shown, and writes a few small steps for your morning, afternoon or evening.

  3. Tick them off

    Start with two-minute versions. They grow as the habit sticks, one day at a time.
